Michael Turk, professionally known as DJ Mic Magic, is a highly skilled and experienced disc jockey, music producer, and audio engineer.

With over 20 years in the industry, Michael has had the opportunity to perform at a variety of events. His extensive background in live performances, event production, and audio engineering allows him to create a dynamic and engaging atmosphere for any occasion.

Michael holds a Bachelor of Arts from Plattsburgh State University and a Graduate Certificate in New Media Design from the University at Buffalo. His academic background, combined with years of hands-on experience, has equipped him with the technical expertise to mix and produce high-quality sound for live events, post-production mastering, and digital recording. In addition to his work as a DJ, Michael has also served as an on-air personality for WQKE and WARP Radio, where he operated the broadcast board, curated playlists, and engaged with listeners.

He has opened for the internationally renowned rock band 3 Doors Down at Plattsburgh State University and appeared as a DJ for legendary hip-hop artists Sugar Hill Gang and Melle Mel in Ithaca, NY


As a wedding and event DJ for Magic's Mobile Music and the American Wedding Group, Michael specializes in curating the perfect soundtrack for every occasion. He understands the importance of setting the right mood and seamlessly blending music to keep the energy flowing, offering clients to create their very own "set lists". He remains at the forefront of the industry by utilizing the latest DJ technology, including Serato, Pro Tools, Adobe Audition, to ensure high-quality sound and flawless transitions. His vast and diverse music library allows him to cater to a wide range of musical tastes, ensuring that every event is both memorable and unique.
