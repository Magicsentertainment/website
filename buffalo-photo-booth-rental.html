Let's Get Started

Capture the Memories with a Photo Booth!

Welcome to our photo booth rental service in Buffalo, NY, where we specialize in adding a touch of fun to your events. Our basic package is a 3 hour rental for $399 and included 60 prints or 120 filmstrips and all photos uploaded to a google drive. Our photo booth is designed to capture unforgettable moments and memories for you and your guests.

Our Photo Booth Packages Include:

  • Choice of Booth
    Select between our modern inflatable booth or classic wall setup to match your event's theme. Our stand-alone wall backdrop is ideal for indoor use.
  • Personalized Photo Templates
    Customize photo layouts to feature event-specific branding or messages. Let us know your color theme or provide a name and logo to add to the template. Templates will need to be approved via email 24 hours within the of the event so they are loaded onto the software in time.
  • High-Quality Prints
    Enjoy instant 4" x 6" prints with up to 60 prints included in our standard package.
  • Fun Props
    Our collection of props is continually growing. Our props included funny hats, mouth cutouts, boa scarves, various sunglasses and frames.
  • Digital Access
    All photos are uploaded to a Google Drive folder for easy viewing and downloading. You can share them to anyone with a link and download them to your computer. Photos are stored on the drive for 30 days after the event.
  • Professional Attendant
    A friendly on-site attendant ensures smooth operation and assists guests throughout the event.

We offer a vareity of props and photo templates to choose from for a personalized touch to your event.
